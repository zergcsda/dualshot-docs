Common questions

The app says my iPhone isn't supported.
Recording with both cameras at once needs an iPhone that supports multi-camera capture: iPhone XS, iPhone XR or later.

Recording stopped by itself.
DualShot stops safely when there is a phone call, when you leave the app, when storage is nearly full, or when the iPhone gets too warm. The footage recorded up to that point is kept. Let the iPhone cool down and try again.

Why is my video 720p?
On some iPhones both cameras can't run at 1080p together, so DualShot uses 720 × 1280 at 30 fps to keep recording smooth.

Where are my clips?
Finished clips are kept inside the app until you delete them. Tap Save to add a clip to Photos. If you denied Photos access, you can allow it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › DualShot.

Can I record without sound?
Yes. Turn the microphone off in the app before recording.

Will deleting the app delete my clips?
Yes. Clips that you haven't saved to Photos or shared are removed with the app.
