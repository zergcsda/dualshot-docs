Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

DualShot does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Camera and microphone

DualShot uses the front and back cameras to record video, and the microphone only when sound is turned on. Video and audio are processed on your iPhone and are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Your clips

Recorded clips are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They leave the app only when you choose to save them to Photos or share them with the iOS share sheet.

Photos

DualShot asks for permission to add videos to your Photos library only when you tap Save. It cannot read or view your existing photos.

Settings

Your preferences, such as theme and layout, are stored on your device only.

Children

DualShot does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
