Record both sides at once.

DualShot films with your iPhone's front and back cameras together and combines them into one vertical video: the scene in front of you and your reaction, in a single take.

Features

  • Picture-in-picture or top/bottom split layout
  • Swap the main and inset camera, move the inset to any corner
  • The live preview matches the final video exactly
  • Mirror the front camera, grid overlay, sound on or off
  • 9:16 video at 1080 × 1920, 30 fps (720p on iPhones that can't run both cameras at 1080p)
  • Clips stay on your iPhone. Save to Photos or share when you choose
  • No account, no ads, no tracking, nothing uploaded

Requires iOS 17 or later and an iPhone that supports multi-camera capture (iPhone XS, iPhone XR or later). Free on the App Store.
